The Inner Take 🎙

A podcast for musicians

The Inner Take is a podcast for musicians — a space to talk about what happens inside us when we make music.

The thoughts and feelings that shape how we play, how we practice, and how we experience our musical lives.

I share my experience as a musician, what I’ve learned along the way, and I talk with other musicians about their journeys.

This podcast is not about technique. It’s about what happens inside us when we make music.
